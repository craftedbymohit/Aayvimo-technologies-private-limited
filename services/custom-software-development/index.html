Frequently Asked Questions

How much does custom software development cost in India?

Cost depends on scope, complexity and integrations. Simple internal tools start modestly; full platforms cost more. After a free discovery call we give you a fixed quote, so you know the investment upfront.

How long does it take to build?

Small tools take a few weeks; larger systems take a few months. We work in agile sprints so you see working software early and often.

Do I own the source code?

Yes. On final payment you receive full ownership of the source code and all project assets.

Do you work with businesses outside Gujarat?

Yes. We're based in Ankleshwar, Gujarat and serve clients across Gujarat and all of India, working remotely with regular updates.
