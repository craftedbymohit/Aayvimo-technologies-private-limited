Cost & Pricing

How much does it cost to build custom software in India?

It depends on scope, complexity and integrations. A simple internal tool or automation is relatively affordable, while a full multi-user platform or ERP costs more. Instead of a fixed price list, we give a clear fixed quote after a free discovery call — so you know the investment before any work begins.

How much does a 20-page website cost?

A 20-page website costs more than a small 5-page site, mainly due to the extra design, content and testing involved. Price also depends on custom design, e-commerce, integrations and SEO. We scope your exact requirements and give a fixed quote after a short call.

How much do software development services typically cost?

Costs range widely based on what you need — from a focused tool to a large platform. The honest answer is that a precise number requires understanding your requirements first, which is exactly what our free consultation is for.

Custom Software

What is custom software development?

Custom software development is building software tailored to a specific business, instead of using generic off-the-shelf products. It fits your exact workflow, automates your processes, integrates with your existing tools, and scales as you grow.

What are custom software development services?

They include requirement analysis, UI/UX design, development, testing, deployment, integration with other systems, and ongoing support and maintenance — a complete solution built around your business rather than a one-size-fits-all product.

Process & Development

What are the 7 stages of the SDLC?

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) commonly has seven stages: 1) Planning, 2) Requirement analysis, 3) Design, 4) Development, 5) Testing, 6) Deployment, and 7) Maintenance. Each stage keeps the project structured, predictable and on time.

How long does it take to build software or a website?

A simple website can take a couple of weeks; a larger web app or custom software takes a few months. We work in agile sprints so you see progress early and often, and we agree on timelines up front.

Do I own the source code?

Yes. On final payment you receive full ownership of the source code and all project assets.

E-Commerce

What does e-commerce development mean? (E-commerce website kya hai?)

E-commerce development means building an online store or platform that lets a business sell products or services online — including product catalogs, a shopping cart, secure payments and order management. In simple terms, an e-commerce website is a website where customers can browse and buy online.

Do you work with businesses outside Gujarat?

Yes. We're based in Ankleshwar, Gujarat and serve clients across Gujarat and all of India, working remotely with regular updates and calls.
